# ZIVO Cambodia Market Compliance Notice

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** users of ZIVO in Cambodia

> **Working template notice:** This notice describes ZIVO's intended operating controls. It is not legal advice and does not confirm that ZIVO holds a particular licence, insurance product, or payment authorisation. Market-specific counsel review and signed addenda control before publication.

## 1. What this notice does

The ZIVO legal library was written first for a United States entity, and several of its pages state United States or European rules — California privacy, do-not-sell, DMCA, seller-of-travel, class-action waiver, GDPR. This notice states what applies to you **in Cambodia**, and it controls over any of those pages where they conflict.

Where Cambodian law gives you a greater protection than any ZIVO policy, Cambodian law controls. Nothing in the ZIVO terms waives a right that cannot lawfully be waived.

## 2. Cambodia legal topics we monitor

For Cambodia operations ZIVO maintains a counsel-reviewed register covering the **Law on Electronic Commerce (2019)**, the **Law on Consumer Protection (2019)**, the **Law on Telecommunications (2015)**, and applicable civil, criminal, tax, competition, payment, licensing, and advertising rules.

Cambodia’s personal-data-protection law is a monitoring item while the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications develops and consults on draft legislation. A draft is not treated as enacted law. ZIVO nevertheless applies purpose limitation, data minimisation, access control, retention limits, and incident response now.

## 3. Choice of law

Some ZIVO pages name the State of Delaware as governing law, because the contracting entity is formed there. A choice-of-law clause does not remove the protection of the consumer law of the country where you live. If you are a consumer in Cambodia, the mandatory consumer protections of Cambodian law remain available to you regardless of that clause, and this notice records that position.

## 4. Price, currency, and what you are charged

- Prices are shown before you confirm, including any platform fee, delivery fee, or tax that applies to your order.
- Where a price is shown in both USD and KHR, the KHR figure is a conversion for display. The currency actually charged is the one shown at the moment of confirmation.
- A charge that differs from the confirmed total must be explained on the receipt, with the reason and a transaction reference.
- No fee may be added after confirmation that was not disclosed before it.

## 5. Refunds, cancellations, and disputes

- The refund and cancellation terms that apply are those shown in the flow you booked through, and they are stated before you pay.
- A refund is returned to the payment method used, unless you agree otherwise in writing.
- If a merchant, host, or provider on ZIVO does not deliver what was described, raise it through in-app support. ZIVO records the dispute, the decision, and the reason.
- Using in-app dispute resolution does not remove your right to bring a complaint to a Cambodian consumer-protection authority or a competent court.

## 6. Your data

- We collect what the service you use requires, and keep it only as long as that purpose and applicable record-keeping rules need.
- You may request a copy of your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account through the in-app privacy controls.
- Deletion requests are honoured except where a payment, tax, safety, or legal-hold record must be retained; where that applies, we say so and state what is retained.
- We do not sell personal data.

## 7. Marketing, cookies, and tracking

- Analytics and marketing trackers load **only after you consent**, and consent is recorded per category. Declining leaves the service fully usable.
- You can change or withdraw consent at any time in cookie settings, and withdrawal applies going forward.
- Marketing messages identify ZIVO as sender and carry a working way to stop receiving them.

## 8. Content, accounts, and enforcement

- Content rules and grounds for account action are stated in the acceptable-use and user-conduct policies.
- Except for an urgent safety, security, fraud, or legal hold, ZIVO should give the reason for a restriction and a usable appeal path.
- A good-faith report about safety, discrimination, privacy, fraud, or a legal violation must not be punished as retaliation.
- Intellectual-property complaints are accepted from Cambodia through the same channel as the DMCA page describes, without requiring a United States filing.

## 9. Age

Services that are age-restricted state the minimum age in the flow. Where an age threshold is set by Cambodian law and differs from the figure shown, the legal threshold applies.

## 10. Law-enforcement and emergency requests

ZIVO discloses user data to authorities only on a lawful request that identifies its legal basis, or where there is a genuine emergency involving a risk to life. Requests are logged.

## 11. Changes

Material changes to this notice are published before they take effect and are not applied retroactively to a transaction already completed.

## 12. Contact

Questions about this notice, or a Cambodian legal or consumer-protection matter, should go to the legal contact listed on the ZIVO business-identity page, or through in-app support, which records the request.
